## About the Movie

**Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse** is an animated superhero film released in **2018**.[^1] It follows **Miles Morales**, a teenager who becomes Spider-Man and meets other Spider-People from different dimensions. I like it because it feels *different* from other superhero movies.

## Main Characters

1. **Miles Morales** - the new Spider-Man
2. **Peter B. Parker** - the older, tired Spider-Man
3. **Gwen Stacy** - Spider-Woman
4. **Kingpin** - the main villain

## Why I Love It

- Unique animation style
- Great characters
  - Miles's growth throughout the story
  - The funny dynamic between the Spider-People
- Best soundtrack
